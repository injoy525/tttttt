Introduction & Methodology

This guide compares three major Korean fried-chicken franchise brands: BBQ, BHC, and Kyochon Chicken. The primary comparison criterion is the total number of physical franchise locations operated within South Korea.

Fair Standard Principle: To maintain strict analytical accuracy, all three brands are evaluated using the same standardized base year: 2024. The numbers presented reflect store outlet count only and do not measure total corporate revenue, market valuation, or average sales per individual franchise unit.

Conclusion

Using the standardized 2024 base year and store location count metric, BBQ possessed the largest franchise network in South Korea, followed closely by BHC and Kyochon Chicken. Objective business comparisons require using identical metrics and explicitly stating data base years. Store location count and average revenue per store represent distinct business dimensions and must not be conflated into a single ranking.
